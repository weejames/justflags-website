Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Just Flags is an iPad app for young children to learn the flags of the world. It is made by James Constable (“we”, “us”).

We don't collect any data

Just Flags does not collect, store or share any personal information or usage data. In particular, the app:

  • has no accounts or sign-in
  • has no advertising
  • has no analytics or tracking
  • makes no network connections
  • has no in-app purchases
  • does not access the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location

Everything the app needs, including the flags and the recorded country names, is built into the app and stays on the device.

Children

Just Flags is designed for children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Apple

Apple may collect information when you download apps from the App Store or if you have chosen to share crash reports and diagnostics with developers. That is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. We don't receive any personal information from Apple about who uses Just Flags.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from third parties. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
